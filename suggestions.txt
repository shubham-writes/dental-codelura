Yes — **absolutely showcase all 3**, but I would **not present them as “three effects.”** That makes them sound like gimmicks.

Your real differentiator is that these are **interactive experiences that help dental practices communicate results and build trust.**

### I’d structure the landing page like this

**Hero**

> Digital experiences for dental practices.

Then, fairly early in the page:

### “See what your patients can experience.”

Use a dedicated interactive section with the three experiences displayed as **large, immersive demos**.

**1. Before / After**
The obvious flagship interaction. Make this the largest one.

**2. Your second interaction**
Show it as another interactive card/demo rather than just a screenshot.

**3. Your third interaction**
Same treatment.

The important part: **let visitors actually interact with them.** Don't make the section a gallery of GIFs or screenshots.

---

### Why I think this is especially important for your site

Your hero currently says:

> *“We design websites…”*

That's a fairly common agency claim.

But if, immediately afterward, someone can physically drag a slider and experience something polished, you've **demonstrated the claim rather than explained it**.

That's much more premium.

I'd actually make the section feel almost like a product showcase:

**INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCES**

# Not just a website.

# An experience patients can feel.

Then:

**Before / After**
*Let patients visualize the difference.*

[large interactive demo]

And underneath/alongside:

**Experience 02** | **Experience 03**

---

### One important design decision

Don't put all three in equal-sized cards.

I'd do:

```text
                 INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCES

        See what your patients can experience.

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                      │
│              BEFORE / AFTER                          │
│                                                      │
│        ←────── drag to reveal ──────→               │
│                                                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

       ┌──────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────┐
       │   EXPERIENCE 02  │   │   EXPERIENCE 03  │
       │                  │   │                  │
       │    interactive   │   │    interactive   │
       └──────────────────┘   └──────────────────┘
```

That creates **hierarchy**: the before/after is the recognizable hero interaction, while the other two prove that you have a broader system.

And I'd give this section **more visual richness than the hero**. Your hero should remain elegant and restrained; the interaction section is where you can let the site become more dynamic.

**So yes: make this a major landing-page section, not a small feature buried near the bottom.** It could actually become the section that makes someone think, *“Oh, this isn't another dental web-design agency.”*
